# Đại Long visual art direction

## Brand idea

Long Gốm is Đại Long's ceramic dragon guide. The coiled body and lifted head take their cue from the curves and rising strokes in the original Đại Long logo. Its glazed cobalt blue is the leading brand color; warm ivory glaze, muted celadon, kiln terracotta and limestone provide supporting colors. Keep the existing logo as the wordmark. Use Long Gốm as a character in the page content.

## Character states

- **Welcome:** friendly, open posture and lifted paw; introduce the brand on the home page.
- **Guide:** attentive expression, presenting a small celadon breeze block; accompany product discovery.
- **Proud:** pleased expression, presenting a curved blue roof tile; appear beside the consultation invitation.

Keep the same rounded silhouette, blue-and-ivory glaze, cobalt cloud and lotus motifs, terracotta horns and claws, facial proportions and curled tail in every pose. Do not add lettering, logos or scenery to mascot artwork. Preserve transparent backgrounds.

## Product photography system

Treat the supplied product photo as the authority for object identity, silhouette, glaze and ornamental details. Keep the product recognizable and materially plausible. Use one product per frame and a square editorial still life, unless the source is an installed architectural application. Share warm natural side light, soft shadows, pale limestone or ivory plaster, restrained greenery and generous breathing room. Keep cobalt, celadon, ivory and terracotta present in glaze or quiet accents. Product imagery is photoreal, calm and tactile; the dragon does not appear in product photographs.

### Shared generation context

> Create a premium Vietnamese architectural ceramics editorial photograph. Preserve the supplied Đại Long product's exact form, visible pattern, material and glaze character. Set it in a quiet warm ivory plaster and pale limestone environment, lit by gentle natural side light with a soft realistic shadow and only restrained greenery. Use a balanced palette of logo cobalt, celadon, warm ivory and kiln terracotta. Keep the object clearly visible and materially plausible, with a clean composition and room around it. Photoreal glazed ceramic texture, sophisticated craft catalogue photography. No mascot, text, watermark or unrelated objects.
